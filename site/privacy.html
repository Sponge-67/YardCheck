Privacy policy

This notice describes the data processed by the YardCheck Android closed-test app (package org.yardcheck.app) and its hosted inspection-coordination service. The operator is FilamentFly. Questions: filamentfly@gmail.com.

Information you provide

  • Accounts and profiles: email address (for registered accounts), chosen or generated username, password hash, session credentials, role, optional profile picture and privacy choice. Registration does not confirm mailbox ownership. Guest profiles use a random device secret.
  • Inspections: auction and lot/VIN details, vehicle description, request name, notes, street/yard address, city/country, preferred times, offers, reports, findings, ratings, task history and scheduling information.
  • Photos and videos: listing screenshots, captions and ordering, uploaded evidence, and profile images. Images and videos may contain other personal data that you choose to include.
  • Conversations: messages exchanged with accepted deal participants, message times/identifiers, read markers, message drafts stored on the device, and chat-related activity events.
  • Safety and deletion: reports, blocks, acceptance of rules, moderation decisions, audit history and account-deletion requests.

How information is used and visible

We use your information to authenticate accounts, coordinate inspections, show available requests to eligible inspectors, facilitate accepted-deal communication, process evidence and reports, prevent abuse and respond to deletion or safety requests. Open requests, listing photos and yard locations can be visible to eligible inspectors before booking. Accepted-inspection evidence and private conversations are limited to authorized participants in the app/API. Authorized service administrators can access stored records for operation and moderation.

Public member profiles show username, reputation and a profile photo if available. New profile photos are visible to signed-in members by default; you may restrict them to yourself and established participants. Entered email and service-profile details are shared with accepted deal participants, not all users. Blocking stops new interaction but does not automatically erase previous messages or accepted-work records.

Other services and processing

YardCheck uses Cloudflare to securely route HTTPS traffic to the test backend; Cloudflare and hosting infrastructure may process connection information such as IP addresses and request metadata. Google ML Kit processes OCR and image labels on the device and may collect SDK diagnostics or identifiers according to Google's disclosures. If you explicitly use a VIN lookup, vehicle-related details may be sent to the NHTSA vPIC service. Opening external auction or map links moves you to those services, which have their own privacy practices.

The test backend stores data on the operator's infrastructure. Deal messages are not end-to-end encrypted. Submitted images and inspection videos are stored remotely; unsent drafts and uploads awaiting retry may remain on your device.

Permissions, retention and deletion

The app needs internet access and uses Android's system media/camera selection mechanisms for images and videos. It does not request device GPS location in the supplied manifest. There are no payments or advertisements in this build.

Data is retained while accounts and inspection records are needed to operate the closed test and fulfill accepted work. Automated time-based erasure is not implemented in this test version. An eligible signed-in member can delete their account immediately after confirming the action and re-entering the current password (not required for guest accounts). Active accepted inspections must be completed first. Deletion revokes all sessions, erases account credentials/profile/contact data, buyer gallery uploads and the deleting member’s private message text. Some completed shared inspection evidence and pseudonymous transaction/audit records remain for the other participant and legitimate legal/security obligations. Media cleanup is queued for retry if an unlink fails; the operator must review backups and any other retained records separately.

You can request deletion within the app or via our public deletion instructions. The Android app now provides self-service account deletion for eligible signed-in members. Requests submitted through the public email route are still handled by the operator. Access and correction questions can be sent to filamentfly@gmail.com.

Children and changes

YardCheck is intended for adults coordinating vehicle inspections, not for children. Material changes to this policy will be published here, and any required in-app notification/consent will be handled by the operator.
