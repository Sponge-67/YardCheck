Privacy controls

Request account deletion

You can initiate deletion of your YardCheck account and associated personal data even if you can no longer open the Android app.

Option A — In the Android app

Sign in to your YardCheck account, open your member/profile settings and select Request account deletion. Confirm and submit the request. The app displays a pending state while the operator reviews it.

Option B — Outside the app

Send a request to the operator using the support address below. Include the subject YardCheck account deletion and the account email/username or the guest-account details you can safely provide. Do not send your password, login tokens, or copies of identity documents in the first message. If required, the operator will agree on a proportionate account-ownership verification method.

Email account deletion request

Or email filamentfly@gmail.com directly.

What happens afterward?

  1. The operator locates and verifies the account and acknowledges the request.
  2. Any active accepted work and legitimate retention requirements are reviewed.
  3. Account credentials, profile data and contact information are removed once deletion is fulfilled. Some shared/pseudonymized inspection, message and audit records may need to remain for other participants or legitimate obligations, with an explanation.
  4. The operator records the outcome and replies through the agreed support channel. Submission alone is not proof that data has already been erased.

Read the privacy policy for data categories and retention limitations. The test backend does not automate backup/media expiry; the operator must fulfil these obligations.
