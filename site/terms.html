Terms and community rules

YardCheck is a closed-test vehicle-inspection coordination service operated by {FilamentFly}. Contact filamentfly@gmail.com for support or safety concerns.

Scope of the test

The app allows buyers and inspectors to post inspection requests, propose visits and prices, coordinate accepted work through chat, upload evidence and review reports. It does not process payments or provide escrow, identity verification, verified auction inventory or guaranteed yard admission. An inspection is a limited visual check, not a warranty or guarantee of vehicle condition.

Acceptable use

  • Share only content and vehicle images you have permission to use. Do not upload someone else's sensitive personal information.
  • Do not post fraudulent listings, threats, harassment, misleading inspection findings, false evidence or unlawful content.
  • Use fresh evidence for the correct vehicle and disclose limitations or checks you could not perform.
  • Confirm the vehicle, auction yard's access rules, the inspector's identity and visit arrangements independently. Never send passwords, codes or pressured off-platform deposits.
  • Use the report and block tools for scams, harassment, unsafe requests or privacy issues. The operator may review reports and restrict accounts or posts.

Profiles, reports and disputes

Ratings and profile photographs are member-supplied signals, not professional verification. Inspection times and quotes are agreed by participants; the operator does not collect fees in this pilot. Messages and evidence are recorded to support accepted work. Buyer confirmation is distinct from the inspector's report submission. The operator reviews complaints but does not promise guaranteed dispute resolution or response times.

Account closure

You may initiate an account-deletion request in the app or through the public deletion page. Active accepted inspection obligations may need to be resolved before deletion can be fulfilled. Some pseudonymous audit or shared inspection history may need to be retained as described in the privacy policy.
